# Privacy notice additions for merchant review

Draft only. Confirm legal entity, jurisdiction, contact address, retention requirements and third-party account regions with the merchant or counsel before publishing. Shopify's own policy remains the final customer-facing source until approved.

When optional tracking is enabled, Project Beej asks visitors for separate analytics, advertising and preference choices. Accept, Reject and Settings are offered with equal prominence. No analytics or advertising tags or denied-consent pings load before opt-in. Visitors can reopen settings from the site footer; withdrawing consent clears the affected local identifiers, synchronizes the checkout consent reduction, and reloads the page.

With analytics consent, the storefront may send page, product, list, cart, checkout-stage, search, newsletter-result, contact-click and Web Vitals events. With advertising consent, approved campaign click identifiers and advertising events may be used by the merchant's configured advertising platforms. Newsletter opt-in is independent and only records marketing permission after the visitor checks the separate email-updates box.

Shopify processes product, cart, checkout, payment and order data. This implementation does not send customer email or phone for conversion matching and does not use session replay. When configured, a separate merchant-owned Supabase project stores consent, signed checkout context, minimal order/refund correlation and retry state. Planned retention is 30 days for campaign attribution and identifiers, 7 days for IP/user-agent and delivery payloads, and 180 days for consent and minimal order/refund correlation. The merchant must confirm these periods and update any applicable Shopify/Google/Meta disclosures before enabling the integrations.

For privacy requests, contact the merchant using the published contact details. Add the legal entity name, mailing address, privacy contact, effective date, applicable rights, and processor details before this draft replaces the Shopify policy.
